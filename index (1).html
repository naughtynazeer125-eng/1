About me

I am a B.Sc. Zoology graduate moving into data analytics. Studying science taught me to ask careful questions, check my assumptions, and let evidence lead the conclusion. I now apply that habit to business data.

I enjoy finding the story inside a dataset, building interactive dashboards that make it easy to read, and analyzing business performance so that teams can make decisions with confidence.

I am an entry-level candidate, and my portfolio is made up of demonstration projects built on sample or personal datasets. I am looking for an entry-level Data Analyst or Power BI Developer role where I can keep learning and contribute from day one.

Employee Attendance & Salary Analysis

Demonstration project

Analyzes attendance, leave, late arrivals, incentives, salary deductions and overtime to show how attendance behavior connects to pay. Presents KPIs, attendance trends, incentive analysis and salary insights.

Business questions

  • Which employees receive the highest overtime payments?
  • Which employees earn the highest incentives?
  • Where do salary losses come from: absence, leave or late arrivals?
  • How does attendance change over time?
  • Microsoft Excel
  • Power Query
  • Power BI
  • DAX
Business rules and assumptions
  • The analysis uses a sample or personal dataset. No real employee data is included.
  • Definitions for late arrival, overtime, incentive and deduction follow the rules written in the project documentation in the repository.
  • Salary deductions are derived from the attendance and leave records in the dataset.
  • Results describe the sample data only and are not a claim about any real organization.
